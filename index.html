Twootz Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version: Twootz collects nothing. Your child's voice stays on the device, is never saved, and is never sent anywhere.

Who we are

Twootz is made by Knox Johnston ("we"). This policy covers the Twootz app for iPhone and iPad.

What Twootz does with sound

When the big red button is held (or tapped once), Twootz uses the device's microphone to listen for up to 10 seconds. The sound is analyzed right on the device to measure its loudness, timing, and pitch, and Twootz then plays back a toot that follows it.

  • The sound is kept only in the device's memory, only long enough to play it back.
  • It is never saved to a file, never uploaded, and never shared with anyone, including us.
  • The microphone is used only while Twootz is listening, never in the background.

What we collect

Nothing. Twootz does not collect, store, or share any personal information, from children or anyone else. That includes names, email addresses, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers, and usage data.

No ads, no tracking, no internet

  • Twootz has no advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • Twootz has no analytics, tracking, or third-party code of any kind.
  • Twootz has no accounts or sign-in.
  • Twootz never connects to the internet.

Children's privacy

Twootz is designed for young children. Because it collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for a parent to review, correct, or delete. The only link out of the app, which opens the iPhone or iPad Settings to turn the microphone on, sits behind a grown-up gate that must be held for two seconds.

Microphone permission

The first time Twootz listens, iOS asks for permission to use the microphone. You can change this at any time in the device's Settings → Twootz. If the microphone is off, Twootz still plays random toots, so the app keeps working.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Twootz will not begin collecting data without updating this policy first.
